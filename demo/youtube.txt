DESCRIPTION
AgentPay is an AI payments agent for freelancers and small sellers. You say what you need in plain words, and it does the PayPal work: invoices, multi-client invoice plans, checkout links, "who hasn't paid me?" summaries, reminders and payouts. Anything that sends money or messages a customer waits for your approval first.

Everything in this video runs on the live app against the PayPal sandbox, with Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) as the brain. All customers are demo addresses (name.demo@example.com).

Live demo: https://agentpay-4lga.onrender.com
Code: https://github.com/Rahulrachu/agentpay
